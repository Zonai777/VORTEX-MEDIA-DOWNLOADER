What you get

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

No browser extensions. No sketchy websites. A clean, fast, beautiful desktop app that just works.

🎞️
Made for editors
One-click presets for After Effects / Premiere (MOV · H.264) and Pro editing (MOV · ProRes). Constant frame rate and synced audio, so files import cleanly every time.
💎
True original quality
Detects the exact codec, format, resolution and frame rate the site sends, and saves that stream untouched. No re-encode, no quality loss.
🧩
Every format & codec
AVI, MP4, MOV, MKV and WEBM with H.264, H.265, AV1, VP9, ProRes 422 / HQ / 4444, DNxHR and Uncompressed. Each option shows if it plays in Windows, AE and Premiere.
🎧
Studio audio
MP3 320, WAV up to 32-bit float, AIFF and FLAC 24-bit, M4A and Opus, or keep the original track. Every format has a "Best" option.
📐
Up to 4K, any frame rate
Every resolution the site offers, plus 2K / 4K upscaling. Keep the source frame rate or convert to 23.976, 24, 25, 29.97, 30, 50, 59.94 or 60 fps. HDR is tone-mapped so colors look right.
✨
Liquid Glass UI
Frosted glass panels, glowing orbs and five themes (Default, Neon, Rose, Gold, Frost). Powered by yt-dlp and FFmpeg, kept up to date automatically.
